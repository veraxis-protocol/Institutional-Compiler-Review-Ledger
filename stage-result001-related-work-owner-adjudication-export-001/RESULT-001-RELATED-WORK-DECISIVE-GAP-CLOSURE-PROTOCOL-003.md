## 3. Reading discipline applied

**Status separation (§13).** Each contemporary source is classified as `MEASURED_IMPLEMENTATION`, `PROOF_OF_CONCEPT`, `ARCHITECTURE_PROPOSAL` or `PLANNED`, and a planned capability is never compared as though measured. Mandato states its own status plainly — "MANDATO is a system under construction … empirical results are defined as a falsifiable plan rather than reported" — and is read accordingly.

**Semantic, not lexical comparison (§6, §10).** Compared as *distinct* until the text shows otherwise: credential validity ≠ token expiry ≠ mandate validity ≠ delegation validity ≠ policy version ≠ rule effective date ≠ rule supersession ≠ institutional competence ≠ actor authority ≠ authorization result. A signed delegation is not mapped to A1/A6/A10; permit/deny is not mapped to epistemic support; a time-bounded credential is not mapped to rule currentness.

**Term-level absence as evidence.** Where a full text is available, the absence of a concept is evidenced by regex over the extracted text and reported with its count, not asserted.
